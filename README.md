# Osvyn Mobile

Osvyn Mobile is the Android app for Muse users with many accounts: up to 10 Muse accounts in one app, every account in its own isolated profile with its own login session.

**Version 1.3.0 · Android only · MIT License · FREE**

## How to get the APK

The APK is not stored in this repo, and neither is the app source. The APK is delivered only in the Osvyn Telegram bot, after your star screenshot is verified:

1. Follow the channel: https://t.me/osvynofficial
2. Star this repo
3. Open the bot: https://t.me/osvynofficialbot — The Product → Osvyn Mobile → "I have starred it", then send a screenshot of your star
4. After verification, the APK (v1.3.0) is sent to you in the bot chat
